What the bot does

The bot may read public Discord messages in approved channels, extract gift-code information, store that information locally, and attempt to redeem codes for users who have registered their Kingshot player information.

What you agree to

  • Use the bot only for lawful and intended purposes.
  • Do not abuse, spam, or overload the bot or its APIs.
  • Do not attempt to bypass rate limits, security controls, or service restrictions.
  • Provide accurate player information when using registration commands.

Data handling

The bot may store limited data locally, such as Discord IDs, Kingshot player IDs, kingdom IDs, gift-code values, and expiry timestamps. This information is used only to support the bot's intended functionality.
